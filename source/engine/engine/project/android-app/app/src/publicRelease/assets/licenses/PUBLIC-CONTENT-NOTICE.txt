FreeDOS Pocket - content notice
===============================

games, personal files, test downloads, caches, credentials, or separately
collected shareware.

The C: environment is assembled from FreeDOS packages. FreeDOS is a collection
of separately licensed programs, not one blanket licence. Package APPINFO data
and licence documents remain inside the guest. Any redistributed build must be
accompanied by the corresponding source and notices required by those licences.
