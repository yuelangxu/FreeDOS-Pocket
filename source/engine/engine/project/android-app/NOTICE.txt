FreeDOS Pocket - Personal Preview: Source and Provenance Notice
=============================================================

This project is a personal offline Android appliance. It is not an official
FreeDOS, Limbo, QEMU, Oracle VirtualBox, or Android product release.

Custom integration sources
--------------------------
The following Java files and Android build/configuration files were created or
adapted for this wrapper and are supplied in this project:

  app/src/org/freedos/pocket/MainActivity.java
  app/src/org/freedos/pocket/DosView.java
  app/src/org/freedos/pocket/Engine.java
  app/src/org/freedos/pocket/SessionService.java
  app/src/com/max2idea/android/limbo/jni/VMExecutor.java
  app/AndroidManifest.xml
  app/build.gradle
  build.gradle
  settings.gradle
  gradle.properties
  Prepare-Assets.ps1
  tools/Start-TestEmulator.ps1

The JNI class path/signatures match the Limbo native interface. The custom
integration adds first-launch disk extraction/checksums, independent persistent
mobile disks, native Canvas/RFB display, keyboard translation and IME integration,
full-frame stretching, a special-key toolbar, and best-effort QMP lifecycle control.
These sources are not a claim that any embedded binary or third-party component
has become proprietary or free of its original license obligations.

Private disk contents
---------------------
The bundled system.vdi and software.vdi seeds are private copies of the user's
existing FreeDOS machine, taken after guest shutdown. They may contain separately
licensed installed software, personal configuration, and user-created files.
Public redistribution of this APK or its disk seeds is NOT authorized merely by
the fact that the emulator or operating system is open source. Review the rights
and privacy of every bundled item before distributing the disk images.

The APK is a private development-signed preview, not a hardened store release.
Please consult README.txt for data-loss warnings and actual validation status.
